Good to know

A few quick answers.

Have another question? Add it to your event enquiry and we’ll cover it while planning.

What kinds of events can DJ Suman support?

Weddings, receptions, private celebrations and corporate events. The services section above shows the music and production options available to discuss.

Can we request particular songs or share a do-not-play list?

Yes. Use the event details box in your enquiry to share favourite songs, artists, cultural moments and anything you’d prefer not to hear.

Can I request only some of the production services?

Yes. Select the services you’re interested in; the final setup can be discussed around your event, venue and schedule.

How do I check my event date?

Include your date and venue in the enquiry form. Availability needs to be confirmed with DJ Suman before your booking is final.

Do you travel to events outside your area?

Share the event city and venue. Travel and availability can be reviewed with your enquiry.
